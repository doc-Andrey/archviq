# Evidence levels used by ARCHVIQ

Every public output must identify the epistemic status of a claim.

- **MEASURED** — directly calculated from the pinned input dataset or explicitly supplied user data.
- **MODELED** — generated by the frozen mathematical transformation/cascade.
- **HYPOTHESIZED** — mechanistic mapping that has biological or computational motivation but is not established as a causal effect in this system.
- **EXPERIMENTAL** — exploratory downstream interpretation requiring independent validation.

A chain may contain several levels. For example, a SILSO percentile may be MEASURED/MODELED, a mapping to a candidate neural circuit HYPOTHESIZED, and a somatic implication EXPERIMENTAL.
