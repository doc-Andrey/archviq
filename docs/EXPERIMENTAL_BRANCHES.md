# Experimental downstream branches

## Somatic branch

The branch is retained in the product as an explicitly **EXPERIMENTAL** research direction.

It may describe exploratory regulatory susceptibility in domains such as autonomic/cardiovascular, metabolic, endocrine/thyroid, immune/allergic, respiratory, and GI/visceral regulation.

It must **not**:
- diagnose disease;
- state that a disease will occur;
- convert a solar/SSN feature directly into a disease label;
- replace medical evaluation.

The intended causal form is:

`developmental signal hypothesis -> processing/regulatory calibration -> susceptibility hypothesis -> lifetime context/exposures -> observed phenotype`

## Psychophysiology / psychosomatic branch

This branch is also **EXPERIMENTAL**. It studies whether processing parameters such as salience, hysteresis, re-entry, recovery and state persistence can help explain individual physiological responses to stress or sustained cognitive/emotional states.

It must be expressed as a hypothesis about dynamics, not as a moral, psychiatric or diagnostic label.

## Moral neutrality

ARCHVIQ models properties of a computational/control architecture. It does not infer whether a person is a criminal, saint, good/bad person, genius, dangerous person, or socially valuable person.

`processing architecture != behavioral or moral identity`
