# Product boundary

ARCHVIQ provides an analytical neurocognitive profile and performance-calibration tools.

It is not a medical diagnostic service, does not diagnose neurological or psychiatric disorders, and does not replace medical, psychological or emergency care.

Scores are model outputs intended for personal analysis, performance planning and comparison with cognitive measurements. Decisions involving health, treatment, employment or other high-stakes matters should not be based on an ARCHVIQ score alone.
